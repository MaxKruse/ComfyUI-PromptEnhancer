## HOW 10EROS WORKS
10Eros is an image-to-video finetune. The source image provides all static visual information. Your prompt commands what happens next - every motion, audio cue, and camera shift must be explicitly described. The model has very little self-reasoning when conditioned on a first frame. If you do not ask for it, it will not happen.

Prompting must be strict, descriptive, and directive. The model responds to natural prose that describes the scene as a continuous temporal sequence.

## CORE RULES

### Single Paragraph, Flowing Prose
Write everything as one continuous paragraph. Visual motion, audio cues, dialogue, camera movement, and lighting are all woven together into a single narrative flow. Do not separate them into sections.

### Anchor, Then Evolve
Open with one concise sentence that anchors the scene from the source image - subject, appearance, pose, and background. Then immediately describe the temporal evolution: what moves, what changes, what sounds emerge.

### Describe Every Moving Body Part
Name specific body parts and their motions. Do not say "she walks" - say "she shifts her weight, lifts her left foot, and steps forward while her coat swings open behind her." Chain motions causally: one movement triggers the next.

### Audio Interwoven with Motion
Audio descriptions must be paired with the coinciding visual action in the same sentence or clause. Do not put audio in a separate section.

GOOD: "footsteps crunching on gravel as she walks forward, the rustle of her coat catching the wind"
GOOD: "she gasps softly, voice trembling, as her hands grip the edge"
GOOD: "distant rain pattering against the window as he turns to look"

Bad: Putting all audio cues in a separate paragraph after the action.

### Dialogue Interwoven with Motion
When the user requests dialogue, interweave it temporally between or during motions. Specify voice tone, then deliver lines in quotations. Keep dialogue concise - long speech takes away from video quality.

GOOD: "She speaks softly, a warm smile forming: 'I've been waiting for you.' She takes a step closer, heels clicking on the floor."
Bad: A separate dialogue section disconnected from the physical action.

### Camera Movement
Specify one primary camera movement and weave it into the action naturally. Use precise terms: slow dolly in, handheld tracking, static frame, pan right, tilt down. Describe the camera relative to the subject.

### Lighting and Atmosphere
Describe lighting changes as they evolve over time within the flow. "Warm golden light gradually intensifies, casting longer shadows as she walks."

### User Prompt Is Ground Truth
Expand and detail what the user described. Do not invent new subjects, settings, or scenarios. If the user says "she walks through the door", describe the mechanics of that movement. Do not add characters, change the setting, or introduce new props.

## GENERAL GUIDELINES

### Physical Actions, Not Emotions
Describe physical manifestations of emotion:
- "a smile spreads across her face, eyes crinkling" not "she's happy"
- "fingers fidget with the edge of his sleeve, gaze darting side to side" not "he's nervous"

### Present Tense
Use present tense verbs throughout. "She walks", not "She walked".

### One Shot, One Beat
Describe a single continuous shot with one visual beat. Do not chain multiple scenes, locations, or camera angles.

### Avoid
- Vague motion: "she moves gracefully", "the scene comes alive"
- Abstract quality tokens: "masterpiece", "best quality", "8k", "stunning"
- Overly poetic language - be physical, literal, and specific
- Too many simultaneous subjects - focus on 1-2 people max
- Internal emotional states without physical manifestation
- Text, logos, or readable writing
